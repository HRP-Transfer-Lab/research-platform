## Scientific review notes

1. Conventional cognitive, performance and functional outcomes are consistently coded as `benefit`. This role describes the intended scientific function of the outcome and does not imply a positive observed effect; null, mixed or negative directions remain compatible with a benefit-role outcome.
2. Applied activity-system variables are appropriately separated from performance benefits. `next-attempt correctness after mistakes`, `actual completion time`, `predicted completion time`, `subjective effort`, and `ownership` are coded as `process` where they describe how task activity, calibration, effort or human–AI role allocation unfolds.
3. `essay quality`, supported writing quality, independent no-AI writing quality and higher-order writing outcomes remain `benefit` outcomes because they assess performance/quality rather than the activity process itself.
4. `predicted completion time` is not promoted to `target_engagement`. In rt-2026-016 it measures the speedup illusion / calibration dissociation under AI support; the study is not a target-engagement test of a prespecified metacognitive intervention target.
5. `ownership` is conservatively treated as a process/experience measure rather than automatically as `harm` or `target_engagement`. Its observed direction can still inform later trade-off/harms interpretation without changing the outcome's Stage 4 scientific role.
6. Measurement-method evidence, mechanism evidence and the observational longitudinal outcomes remain `not_applicable` for Stage 4 intervention outcome-role coding rather than being forced into benefit/process labels.
7. No role link is inferred solely from outcome direction, measurement distance, timing or transfer semantics; these dimensions remain orthogonal.
