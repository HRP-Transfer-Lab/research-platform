## Decision

Stage 7 is verified.

The Registry no longer treats source-linked `quality_assessment` rows as the canonical v1.1 quality architecture. Scientific appraisal is now separated by the subject actually being judged:

```text
STUDY / REPORT
    ↓
study methodological / reporting / review / measurement appraisal

OUTCOME × CONTRAST / EFFECT
    ↓
result-specific risk of bias

PROPOSITION / SYNTHESIS OUTCOME
    ↓
body-level certainty / GRADE — deferred to Stage 8

INTERVENTION DELIVERY
    ↓
fidelity / implementation — kept separate for Stage 10 expansion
```

The governing invariant is:

> **Study/report quality != result-specific risk of bias != body-level certainty != implementation/fidelity.**

## Result-specific RoB integrity

`result_risk_of_bias_assessment` is anchored to a normalized outcome and may additionally identify the scientifically relevant Stage 5 contrast and/or Stage 6 effect estimate.

Database guards enforce:

```text
outcome study == contrast study
effect outcome == RoB outcome
effect contrast == selected RoB contrast, where specified
```

Stage 6 `source_level_synthesis` effects cannot be forced into a trial-result RoB subject. Review/meta-analytic methodology belongs to an appropriate review/study or later body-level object rather than a fabricated trial contrast.

Domain judgements use real foreign-key subjects: each domain row belongs to exactly one study-quality assessment or one result-RoB assessment.

## Workbench verification

The Evidence Workbench now exposes typed Stage 7 review rather than one generic source-level quality form.

The Stage 7 interface separates:

```text

- assessment status
- assessment kind
- registered compatible framework
- version
- overall judgement
- assessor/date/notes
- provenance/review status

RESULT RISK OF BIAS
- outcome/result
- Stage 5 contrast where applicable
- Stage 6 effect estimate where applicable
- RoB framework
- estimand/result scope
- overall judgement
- domain judgements
- provenance/review status

BODY CERTAINTY
- explicitly deferred to Stage 8
```

The UI reads framework applicability from `assessment_framework_definition`, preventing the form vocabulary from drifting from the database subject rules.

GRADE is not offered as a Stage 7 source/study/result write option.

The previous generic `Add appraisal` form was removed. Historical source-linked quality rows, if ever present, are displayed only under **Historical quality compatibility**.

The Workbench build gate was completed before the final clean/synced implementation state was recorded.
