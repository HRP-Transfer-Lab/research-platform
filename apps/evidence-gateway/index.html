How to read the Gateway

Human approved records belong to the current approved seed release. Research watchlist records are screening-level research leads awaiting human verification and deeper extraction. Boundary / excluded records are retained so scope decisions remain transparent.

What it does not claim

The Gateway is an evidence-discovery and CSI evidence-supply product. It is not itself a systematic review, medical advice, treatment selector, or a licence to turn screening records into decision-facing claims.
