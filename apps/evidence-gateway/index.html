How to read this demo

Approved records belong to the current human-reviewed seed release. Provisional records are assistant-researched or assistant-adjudicated screening records awaiting later human verification and deeper extraction. Boundary / excluded records are retained to make scope decisions transparent.

Intended use

This Gateway is an evidence-discovery and product-development demonstration. It is not a systematic review, medical advice, or a source of independently approved clinical claims.
